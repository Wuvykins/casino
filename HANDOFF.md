# Handoff — The Casino (Nic's Hoyle-style casino)

Give this file and the project zip to a fresh chat and it can pick up where the last one left off.

## What this is
A private, Hoyle Casino 1999–style casino for Nic's friends and family, played in landscape on iPhone/iPad as a web app.
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS ES modules, no build step. One save on the device (`localStorage`, key `casino.save.v1`).
Nic designs all the art, voice lines and music himself and sends them in chat; the assistant integrates them.

## How it's tested and shipped
- Live test link (a multi-file Claude artifact): https://claude.ai/artifact/8cvLLqFKsWdH7V3HzkwN1Q
  Republish `artifact.html` **to that same URL** with a `files` map of the changed files (js/**, css/app.css, assets/**).
  The artifact tool refuses to overwrite files it hasn't seen in the session: read them from the artifact first, then publish.
  Nic does not want zip downloads for routine testing — the live link is the loop.
- Phone install: it's a PWA (manifest + `sw.js` generated by `tools/build_sw.py`, which must be re-run after any file change). Hosted on GitHub Pages from Nic's repo; Add to Home Screen in Safari; runs offline.
- Local: `python3 serve.py` then open the printed address on the phone (same Wi-Fi). `index.html` is the local entry.
- Tests: `npm test` (poker, blackjack incl. 1M-hand house-edge check, cribbage, farkle, luck, AI sims),
  `npm run test:ui` (Playwright play-throughs of hold'em and blackjack; screenshots land in tests/shots/).
  Playwright launches `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome` in the cloud box; adjust if elsewhere.

## Rules Nic set (don't break these)
- Accessibility for his 65-year-old dad: big cards, big cutout portraits (no circles), big chips with numbers, big result tags and hand names, big bank card.
- Don't deal the next hand until the player presses Deal. Fun win screen with a chip stack; BIG WIN = net gain ≥ 25 BB (hold'em) or blackjack / ≥ 8× min bet (blackjack).
- Character picker: real people first by default; real people show name only (bots keep taglines). Bots: marty, dot, rico, vee.
- Cast personalities: Nic = best, tight, only calls with a good hand. Freddy = aggressive, bluffs, shoves. Kurtis = terrible, calls with nothing, never aggressive.
  Nathaniel = conservative, never bluffs, calls with any pair "within reason". Mom = between Nathaniel and Freddy, jolly, says "Damnit." on a big loss / bad beat. Courtney = skill 4/10, calls too much (persona.station 1.6); first character with a full recorded voice set (assets/voice/courtney/*.mp3). Ken = banker.
- Hold'em feel (Nic, 2026-09-20): a family game, not a card room. ai.js has FAMILY_SKILL 0.8 (everyone plays below their dial) and LIMP_URGE 0.2 (+ (1-tight)*0.4): when nobody has raised, most people call the blind to see a flop (Nic ~40% VPIP). Freddy has persona.checkRaiser 1: whenever it checks to him postflop he bets, with the `tooMuchChecking` line.
- Cribbage ability is a separate dial, persona.cribSkill: Mom .85 (the family shark), Nic .45, Nathaniel .45, Freddy .2, Courtney .2, Kurtis .12; bots use their poker skill.
- Leaving: 'Leave table' always works. Mid-hand/mid-game it asks Stay / finish first / Leave now (askLeave in components.js). Leave now = fold (hold'em, pot stays), forfeit the bet (blackjack) or the stake (cribbage, farkle). Nic: nobody should ever have to close the app to get out.
- Luck (Nic, 2026-09-21; js/core/luck.js): sporadic, NOT tied to session start/return — Nic explicitly did not want "welcome back" reinforcement loops. Hold'em, per hand: 3% "family" deck (everyone has something, big pot, human best at river), 8% "value" deck (human best at river, 1–2 opponents hold real second-best hands so they call/raise legitimately), 12% just better hole cards (everything else honest). Rigged hands need human preflop strength .28–.8, ≥ pair on the flop, ≥ two pair at the river, strictly best; they only pay off if he stays in. Blackjack: 6% of deals make 20/21, 15% of hits on 12–16 land 17–21, 15% of dealer draws bust while he stands on 12–16. Everything else is a straight deal so he still loses from normal play. Constants in LUCK; tests/luck.test.mjs. The human is `HUMAN` ('you') — luck never applies to companions/bots.
- Ken's line is exactly "Ken gave you {amount}, go have fun." — no "Damn it", no "Bank Manager" label.
- Voice lines are `{ text, file }` pairs per line; recorded lines are preferred when present. The text bubble ALWAYS shows, even with a recording (Nic's rule).
- Persona `chatty` (default 1) scales how often a character talks; `taunt` trigger = trash talk when you fold to them / bust / count nothing. Freddy is the trash-talker (chatty 1.8).
- Recording plan: one folder per person, files `NN-trigger.m4a` per the list in voice-file-names.md; Nic edits the wording and sends the list with the audio. Clips are cut/normalised with ffmpeg (loudnorm I=-16) and saved as MP3 — the artifact host does not serve .m4a. If one long recording comes in, split it by silence (energy envelope, gap >= 0.4 s) and match segments to the list in order. Files: `assets/voice/<id>/<file>`.
- Sounds (all Nic's recordings in assets/sfx): deal.mp3 per card, check.mp3 (two taps), call.mp3 (short "ante up" chip drop: only for limping in / calling the blind, and quietly when blinds are posted), raise.mp3 (bets, raises AND calling a raise — Nic's call), allin.mp3, dice.mp3, lose = his negative.mp3 pitched down; turn change/fold = synth tap.
- Music + room sound (audio.js, all WebAudio: element → lowpass → gain, because iPhone Safari ignores element.volume): his dad's songs (assets/music/song-1..13.mp3; recipe for new ones: trim to the music onset (energy envelope), fade in .5 s / out 1.5 s, highpass 80, lowpass 2200 p=2 + lowpass 4000 p=2, loudnorm I=-31, mono 64k — matches songs 1–2), shuffled with a 3 s gap (SONG_GAP); lobby level = musicVolume setting (0.7), at a table 0.4× and lowpassed at 1400 Hz (DUCK, TABLE_MUFFLE). Lobby room sound = assets/music/lobby-room.mp3 (Nic's poker_room clip made into a seamless 50.8 s loop, -24 LUFS) at ROOM_LEVEL 0.4× music volume; plays only on the lobby floor (fades out on duck(true) — select screens and games), toggle 'Lobby room sound' (settings.roomSound). music.debug() shows the live graph state. music.skip() = next song with no gap; "Skip song ⏭" button sits beside the Music toggle in Settings (reachable mid-game via the gear).
- Table art: assets/img/table/felt-holdem.png is Nic's oval poker table cut out onto transparency (from his 1774×887 render; surround removed with an ellipse mask). `.holdem .felt.has-art` draws it at 75%×73.3% centred at 50%/54% — exactly where the old drawn .rail sat — so the seats stay around the table on the dark floor (Nic: people must not sit on the felt). On phones (max-height 500px) it is 75%×66% at 50%/83%: lower and flatter so the top seat’s name plate just meets the rail instead of covering it. Blackjack and cribbage fall back to the same PNG stretched 100%/100% (`.felt.has-art`) until he paints their own.
- Startup (Nic, 2026-09-21): phones won't play sound before a tap, so the app first tries video.play() unmuted on load: if the browser allows it (most desktops) the intro just runs; if it refuses (iPhone/iPad) the splash gets .gate and shows a pulsing "Tap to enter" on the intro's first frame (assets/img/splash.jpg); that tap starts Nic's 12 s intro video (assets/video/intro.mp4, 1536×768 h264 + aac, src directly on the <video> so errors reach us) WITH sound while boot() loads (~1 s). Logo forms at normal speed, then once loaded the rest runs at 2.2× (INTRO_LOGO_AT 5.2 / INTRO_FAST) → ~8 s total. 'Tap to skip' after 2.5 s. If nothing decodes within 4 s (or on error) the game just opens. #splash gets .ready when the tap listener is live, .started after the tap, .can-skip later — the smoke tests wait for .ready, tap, then tap again on .can-skip, and repeat after every page.reload().
- ffmpeg gotcha: put `-ss` BEFORE `-i` when trimming mp3s or you get silence.
